What is Purikura?

Purikura are Japanese photo booths that allow you to take photos, then edit and decorate them! (the name comes from 'print club') This doesn't work quite the same, but who doesn't like making their art look pretty? Not me!

Also, all drag-and-drop assets were made by yours truly (Sev sevennn-39) on various art programs (drawing w/ a mouse is pain yay)!
